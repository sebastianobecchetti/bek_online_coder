BEK Online Coder

Encodes a 3D Gaussian Splatting .ply scene into the compact .bek container, or decodes a .bek back to .ply — entirely inside this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded anywhere: the file never leaves your machine. Direction is detected from the file's own bytes, not its name.

Before you load a large file:
  • Encoding is CPU-heavy (RVQ / k-means training dominates the time). Expect roughly ~5s for 20k splats up to several minutes for 1-2M splats on a modern laptop — more on slower machines. Decoding is fast (no k-means): seconds even for large scenes.
  • The whole input file and several working copies (for decode: the full .ply, which materializes every property as a 32-bit float and is typically 15-25× larger than the source .bek) are held in memory; very large files may exhaust browser tab memory.
  • Keep this tab open and don't put the computer to sleep while it's running.
  • Everything runs locally in a Web Worker — your scene data is never sent to a server.
